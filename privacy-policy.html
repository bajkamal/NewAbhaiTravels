Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. Overview

New Abhai Travels ("we", "us", "our") arranges cars, tempo travellers and buses for hire. This policy explains what information we collect when you enquire about or book a vehicle with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not process any online payments through this website, so we never collect card, UPI, or bank details here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to contact us, such as through a phone call, WhatsApp message, or email. This may include:

  • Your name and contact number or email address
  • Travel details you share with us, such as pickup/drop locations, dates, and preferred vehicle
  • Any other details you voluntarily share to help us plan your journey

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising scripts to profile visitors on this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you share is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and confirm vehicle availability and rates
  • Coordinate pickup, drop, and trip logistics
  • Follow up regarding an ongoing or completed booking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share limited trip details (such as your name and pickup point) with the specific driver assigned to your journey, solely to carry out the service you've booked. We do not share your information with any other third party unless required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain booking-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil your trip, handle any follow-up questions, and meet basic business record-keeping needs, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, by reaching out through any of the contact options on this site. We will act on such requests as soon as reasonably possible.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach out to us at newabhaitravels@yahoo.co.in or call +91 80902 02020.
