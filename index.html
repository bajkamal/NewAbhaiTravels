Common Inquiries

What do I need to rent a vehicle?

A valid driving licence, a government photo ID, and a refundable security deposit. For tempo travellers and buses, a driver is included as standard.

Do tempo travellers and buses come with a driver?

Yes, every tempo traveller and bus is rented with a driver included as standard.

What's the fuel policy?

Vehicles are handed over with a full tank and should be returned full. If not, refuelling is charged at the local pump rate plus a small service fee.

Can I cancel or reschedule an enquiry?

Since nothing is paid online, there's nothing to formally cancel. Just message or call us to change plans and we'll adjust or reschedule.

How is the rate confirmed if there's no online checkout?

Rates depend on the vehicle, dates, distance and any add-ons. Enquire for the vehicle you want and we'll confirm the exact total over WhatsApp or a call before you commit.
