Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. How Bookings Work

Since we don't take online payments on this website, every booking is confirmed directly with our team over a call, WhatsApp, or email, and any advance or security deposit is collected and refunded the same way — never through this site.

2. Cancelling a Booking

You can cancel a confirmed booking any time by informing us over a call or WhatsApp. We ask that cancellations be made as early as possible so the vehicle can be released for other customers. The exact notice period and any applicable cancellation charge will be shared with you at the time your booking is confirmed, since these can vary by vehicle type and season.

3. Refund of Advance or Deposit

Where an advance amount has been collected for a booking that is later cancelled within the agreed notice period, it is refunded in full. Where a booking is cancelled after that window, or on the day of travel, we may deduct a reasonable cancellation charge to cover vehicle and driver allocation already made, and refund the remaining balance.

A refundable security deposit collected for a completed trip is returned after the vehicle is checked in, subject to deductions for any damage, traffic violations, or extra charges incurred during the trip as outlined in our Terms & Conditions.

4. Refund Timeline

Once a refund is agreed, it is processed within 5–7 business days to the same account or method the payment was originally made from.

5. Changes on Our End

In the rare case that we're unable to fulfil a confirmed booking due to a vehicle breakdown or other circumstance on our end, we will make every reasonable effort to arrange an alternative vehicle of similar or better standard. If no suitable alternative is available, any advance paid will be refunded in full.

6. Contact Us

To cancel a booking or ask about a refund, call us at +91 80902 02020, message us on WhatsApp, or email newabhaitravels@yahoo.co.in.
